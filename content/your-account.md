# Managing your online account

## Resetting your password

Select "Forgot password" on the login page and follow the link we email you. The link expires
after 30 minutes.

## Updating your details

Change your email address, phone number or postal address under "My details" in your online
account.

## Closing your account

To close your account, call customer service. We will send a final bill for any charges up to
the closing date.
