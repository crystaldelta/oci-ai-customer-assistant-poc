# Understanding your bill

Your bill shows what you owe, when it is due, and how your charges were calculated.

## Service charge

A fixed monthly charge for your plan, whether or not you use the service that month.

## Usage charges

What you pay for anything used beyond your plan's monthly allowance. Your bill lists each charge
with its date.

## Late payment fee

A late payment fee may apply if a bill is not paid by its due date. Contact us before the due date
if you need more time.

## Why your bill went up

Common reasons are usage beyond your allowance, a change to your plan, a one-off charge such as a
replacement device, or the end of a discount period.
